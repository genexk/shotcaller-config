Privacy policy

Shotcaller for iPhone · Last updated 3 November 2026

No data collection

Shotcaller doesn't collect, store or share any personal data on servers of its own. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no account of its own.

Your machine and scale

Shotcaller talks directly from your phone to your own espresso machine and your own scale: it reads the scale's weight and sends commands such as standby to the machine. Nothing it reads from them leaves your phone.

Optional account sign-in

If you choose to sign in with your La Marzocco account, your email and password are sent only to La Marzocco's servers, once, to fetch your machine's token. Shotcaller then signs out and doesn't keep your password; your email stays on your iPhone to fill in next time. After that it contacts La Marzocco's servers only when you ask it to get a token again.

Only if you turn on Cloud features in Settings › Advanced does Shotcaller stay signed in, keeping your password in your iPhone's Keychain, to show your machine's status and send controls through La Marzocco's cloud. Turning it off signs out and deletes the password. Your credentials are never sent anywhere else. La Marzocco's own privacy policy covers that data.

On your device

Your shot history, settings and learned stop timing stay on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Update check

To tell you about updates and about unsupported machine firmware, the app downloads a public configuration file from GitHub Pages and looks the app up on the App Store. These requests send no personal data. GitHub and Apple may log the request, as any web server does.
