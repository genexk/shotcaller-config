What Shotcaller does

Shotcaller reads your scale, predicts when your shot will reach the weight you set, and stops your own espresso machine at that moment, then gets it ready for the next shot. It works with the machines and scales listed in the app under Settings › About. Models marked Experimental haven't been tried on real hardware yet.

Your machine

Shotcaller only sends commands the manufacturer's own app also sends. It doesn't modify your machine or its firmware. A firmware update from the manufacturer, a lost connection or a low battery can stop it from working as expected, so stay with your machine while it brews, as you would without the app. Espresso machines use hot water and steam; follow the manufacturer's safety instructions.
