Shotcaller for iPhone

Shotcaller stops your espresso shot at a target weight. It reads a Bookoo scale and puts a La Marzocco Linea Micra or Linea Mini R into standby at the right moment.

Get help or report a problem

Email getshotcallerapp@gmail.com. Please include your machine model, its firmware version (shown in the La Marzocco app, or in Settings › Machine when Cloud features is on), the app version (Settings › About Shotcaller), and what happened.

Before you ask

  • The line under the Home title says whether a shot can be stopped right now, and what to do if it can't.
  • If the La Marzocco app is open, close it: the machine accepts one connection at a time.
  • If the machine isn't found, close the La Marzocco app, then switch the machine off at its power switch for 10 seconds and back on.

Terms of use · Privacy policy

Shotcaller works with your own machine. It stops a shot with the same standby command as the manufacturer's app, and it doesn't modify the machine or its firmware. Use it at your own risk.
