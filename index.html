Stop every shot at the weight you set.

Pull the paddle as usual. Shotcaller watches your scale, predicts the moment to stop, and stops your machine right on target, even with your phone in your pocket.

Coming soon to theApp Store For iPhone

How it works

Set a target

Choose the grams you want in the cup.

Pull the paddle

Brew exactly as you always do. Shotcaller follows the weight and the flow live.

It stops on target

It stops the machine just before your target, then gets it ready for the next shot.

Made for the way you actually brew

Works with your phone locked

Follow the shot on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and get a notification with the result.

Learns your machine

Every shot teaches it how long your machine keeps pouring after a stop, so it lands closer each time.

No extra hardware

No boards to install and nothing to open up. It talks to your machine and scale straight from your iPhone.

Leaves your scale alone

It only reads the weight. It never tares the scale or runs its timer.

A small coffee corner

Home shows your machine in a little 3D room that follows the time of day, in the colour you choose.

Private by design

No accounts with us, no analytics, no ads. Your shots stay on your iPhone.
